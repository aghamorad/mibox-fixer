What the setup button actually does

It checks whether your box is 32-bit or 64-bit, installs any missing core apps (Hume home screen, TV Live, SmartTube, TV Bro, SlamVision, MATVT mouse helper, and Aerial Views 4K Space & Nature slideshow), turns off the Samsung TV deep-sleep bug so your TV remote always wakes the box, and sets Hume as your home screen.
